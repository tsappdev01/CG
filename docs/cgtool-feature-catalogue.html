RP Transactions

The related party transaction workflow, from raising through approval and governance decision to the register. Each screen is one stage, and a person sees only the stages their role reaches.

Add New Transaction

/rp-transactions/new

Who: anyone with RP Transaction access

Raises a transaction with a related party. The entity and requestor are taken from your own record and locked; the counter-party can only be chosen from the Related Party Master, so a party that has not been declared cannot be transacted with by accident.

  • Pick a counter-party from the Related Party Master
  • Enter value, date of request and the material terms
  • Attach the purchase order, contract or quotation
  • See the pre-check results at submission, pass or fail
  • A value over the delegated authority routes higher automatically

CCAO Review

/rp-transactions/ccao-review

Who: the CCAO, and administrators

Everything approved and waiting on a governance decision. The decision itself is taken at a meeting rather than on screen, so this records it: the deciding body, the meeting date, the members who abstained as conflicted, the compliance conclusion and the minutes as evidence. Approving releases the transaction to the register.

  • Review the transaction, its approvals and its documents
  • Record the deciding body and meeting date
  • Name the members who abstained as conflicted
  • Attach the minutes or approval evidence
  • Approve and release, or reject with remarks

Delegation of Authority

/admin/doa-matrix

Who: administrators

The approval limits that decide where a transaction routes. Each entity's matrix sets, per role, the value up to which that role may approve; a transaction above the limit goes higher. Changing a matrix never re-routes a transaction already in flight.

  • Set approval limits per entity and role
  • Submission routing reads the matrix at the moment of submission
  • Changes are audited and do not disturb transactions in flight
